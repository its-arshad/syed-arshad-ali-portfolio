CabIndians is an online transportation network, operating as Bilwam India Cabs Pvt Ltd, providing affordable rides anywhere and anytime in India with a mobile app, offering 24/7 service with no surcharge on peak hours. Here's a more detailed breakdown:

*   **What they do:** CabIndians connects users with drivers for rides, offering a convenient and affordable transportation option. 

*   **How they operate:** They operate as an online transportation network, allowing users to request rides through a mobile app. 

*   **Services:**

    *   **24/7 Service:** They offer rides anytime, day or night. 

    *   **Ride Now/Ride Later:** Users can request rides immediately or schedule them for later. 

    *   **Zero Surcharge on Peak Hours:** There are no extra charges during peak hours. 

    *   **Variety of Vehicles:** They offer different vehicle types, including Auto, Mini, Sedan, and SUV. 

*   **Location:** They are based in Hyderabad, Telangana. 

*   **Company:** Bilwam India Cabs Pvt Ltd operates as CabIndians. 

*   **Find them:** You can find more information about CabIndians on [Facebook](https://m.facebook.com/cabindians/about/) and [LinkedIn India](https://in.linkedin.com/company/cabindians).
